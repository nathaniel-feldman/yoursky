Privacy

Your Sky is a college search for high school students. This page explains, in plain English, what we collect, why, and how to delete it. Last updated October 7, 2026.

Without an account

  • You can take the quiz and see your sky without signing up.
  • Your answers, GPA and scores stay in your own browser (local storage) so your sky survives a page reload or a sign-in. We don’t receive them unless you ask for Pro data, which sends your answers to our server to calculate results and doesn’t store them.
  • We count a few anonymous events, like “quiz started” or “quiz finished,” to see whether the site works. These have no name, email, account or device identifier attached.
  • If you arrive from a creator’s link, we remember their code in your browser for 30 days so they get credit if you buy Pro.

If you make an account

Accounts are free and optional. You must be 13 or older. We collect:

  • Your email address, to sign you in with a one-time code. No passwords. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address from Google.
  • Your saved skies: your quiz answers, your kept schools and their fit scores, and your first name, majors and home region.
  • Friend connections you make with a friend link, and the name you choose to show friends.
  • Optional academic inputs (GPA, SAT/ACT, family income range), only if you turn on “Remember my GPA, scores and income range.” They are never shown to friends.
  • Purchases: if you buy Pro, our payment provider (Lemon Squeezy) handles payment details. We receive and keep an order record (order number, amount, status, any creator code), not your card details, and we don’t store your name or email from the order.
  • The creator code you arrived with, if any, so the right creator gets credit.

Who can see what

  • Only you can see your account, saved skies and academic inputs.
  • A friend sees your latest saved sky (schools, fit scores, answers, first name and majors) only after you both accept the connection. They never see your grades, scores, budget, income or Pro estimates.
  • There are no public profiles, no search, no messaging and no way for strangers to find you.

What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your data.
  • No ads and no advertising trackers (no Facebook, TikTok or Google ad pixels).
  • We don’t use your data to train AI models or share it with data brokers.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts and saved skies.
  • Lemon Squeezy processes Pro payments as the merchant of record, under its own privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website.
  • Google, only if you choose “Continue with Google.”

Deleting your data

  • Delete any saved sky from your account page.
  • Delete your whole account from Account → Privacy → Delete account. This immediately and permanently removes your account, saved skies, friend connections and academic inputs. Order records stay, with no link to you, because we may need them for refunds and tax records.
  • Clear your browser’s site data to remove anything stored locally.
  • Questions or requests: contact@findyoursky.com.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we’ll update the date above and tell signed-in users.
