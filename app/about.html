Your Sky

About

Your Sky is a college search that starts with you. Answer a few questions and the schools that fit you best pull into closer orbit. There are no rankings and no accounts.

Your privacy

  • No sign-up, no cookies, no tracking.
  • Everything you enter stays in your browser while the page is open. Close it and it's gone.
  • A share link holds only your first name, your kept schools and your answers. It never includes your GPA, test scores, budget or family income.

Where the data comes from

Admission rates, SAT and ACT ranges, costs, net price by family income, graduation rates, earnings and the share of graduates in each field come from the U.S. Department of Education's College Scorecard. School colors, curriculum styles and notes are hand-curated.

Please double-check

  • Cost estimates are based on what students actually paid after grants. Your real cost can be higher or lower; every school has a net price calculator, linked on its detail page.
  • Reach, target and likely are rough guides from public admissions data, not predictions.
  • Deadlines are typical dates and change from year to year. Always confirm on each school's website.

Who it's for

High schoolers figuring out where they might want to go, and anyone helping them. Talk your list over with a counselor, a teacher or someone who knows you.
